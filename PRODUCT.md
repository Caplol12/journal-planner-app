# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion, Lucide React, Supabase

## Users

Persian-speaking individuals, professionals, writers, and students who seek a serene, distraction-free environment for daily reflection, task management, time-blocking, and personal journaling in the Jalali (Solar Hijri) calendar.

## Product Purpose

`journal-planner-app` provides an all-in-one digital planner and notebook ecosystem. It eliminates cognitive friction and chaotic multitasking by combining multiple specialized journals, 24-hour time-blocking (Chronodex and linear schedules), Eisenhower priority matrices, habit tracking, and open-ended reflective writing into a peaceful, deeply focused daily ritual. Success is defined as a user feeling clear-headed, grounded, and motivated to return daily without visual fatigue.

## Positioning

Unlike cluttered enterprise task managers (like Jira or Asana) or superficial habit apps with distracting gamification and neon badges, this product offers an editorial-grade, tactile digital notebook experience rooted in Iranian/Jalali date systems and classical journaling wisdom (similar to paper Hobonichi, Midori, Notion, and Craft).

## Operating Context

- **Daily Ritual**: Morning goal setting (Three Priorities, Time Blocking), evening reflection (Habits, Gratitude, Journaling).
- **Surface Modes**:
  - Dashboard: Overview of personal notebooks and distinct templates.
  - Journal Pages Overview: Chronological and search-driven archive of entries.
  - Active Daily Planner: Focused writing surface with template variations (Sage Daily Planner, One Big Thing, Writing Plans, Celestial Tasks, Custom Templates).
  - Orbit Calendar: Solar Hijri date navigation and astrological rhythm tracking.
  - Admin & Users: Cloud synchronization, team/user management, and profile settings.
- **Environments**: Desktop workspaces, laptops, tablets, and responsive mobile viewports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Preserved Core Logic**:
  - Full local storage fallback and real-time Supabase cloud sync.
  - Jalali (Solar Hijri) date arithmetic, conversion, and formatting.
  - Audio tactile feedback (calm mechanical ticks and stars).
  - Drawing canvas overlay state and stroke persistence.
  - Custom HTML template rendering and bidirectional postMessage synchronization.
  - Light and dark theme switching.
- **Durable Constraints**:
  - RTL-first typography and interface orientation.
  - No loss of user data, input handlers, or state connections during UI overhauls.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone of Voice**: Calm, understated, scholarly, reassuring, and dignified.
- **Visual Stance**: Functional Minimalism. Zero artificial AI clichés (no cards nested in cards, no heavy decorative borders, no shiny cyber-gradients).
- **Physical Metaphor**: Premium uncoated paper, archival notebooks, smooth fountain pens, warm stone and linen.

## Product Principles

1. **Content is the Hero**: The user's words, thoughts, and plans take center stage; the UI melts into the background.
2. **Quiet Tactility**: Interactions, transitions, and audio cues are whisper-soft and deliberate, never loud or jumpy.
3. **Whitespace as Architecture**: Spatial breathing room creates clarity; structure is achieved through hierarchy, whitespace, and gentle value shifts rather than heavy boxes.
4. **Resilient Data Fidelity**: Every keystroke, checkbox toggle, and stroke is safely preserved across local and cloud storage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA text contrast across both light and dark themes.
- Full Persian screen reader support with semantic HTML elements.
- Fluid responsive layouts tailored for mobile, tablet, and desktop without cramped text.
